# Tech Nomads

**Tech Nomads** is a lifestyle and entrepreneurial movement founded by **Timothée Bacher** in 2024. It aims to empower individuals in the tech industry to live and work globally by building small, independent businesses — especially Micro SaaS, AI tools, and digital products.

---

## Origins

Founded by French product manager and digital nomad **Timothée Bacher**, the movement emerged from a desire to simplify the path to location and financial freedom for tech professionals. Tech Nomads represents a shift from traditional employment toward self-directed, remote-friendly entrepreneurship.

---

## Principles

- **Freedom through technology**  
  Leverage modern tools (AI, APIs, low-code platforms) to build scalable products with minimal overhead.

- **Micro over massive**  
  Focus on small, high-impact digital projects rather than chasing venture capital or hypergrowth.

- **Remote by design**  
  Build businesses that are location-independent from day one.

- **Community over hustle culture**  
  Value collaboration, creativity, and long-term sustainability over burnout and grind.

---

## Focus Areas

- Micro SaaS and software tools  
- AI-powered solopreneur projects  
- Digital nomad tools and templates  
- Remote productivity and automation  
- Minimalist, profitable digital business models
